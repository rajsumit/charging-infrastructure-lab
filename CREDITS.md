# Credits

## Course material
This project is a study companion for the NPTEL course **Charging Infrastructure**.

- **Course:** Charging Infrastructure, NPTEL / SWAYAM
- **Instructor:** Prof. Apurv Kumar Yadav, Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee
- **Platform:** NPTEL (National Programme on Technology Enhanced Learning), an initiative of the IITs and IISc, Government of India

The weekly assignment questions (Weeks 1 to 12) in `src/qbank1.js`, `src/qbank2.js`, `src/qbank3.js`
and the question figures in `src/imgs.js` are taken from the course assignments, and the lecture facts
in the topic pages follow the lecture transcripts and slides. All of that material belongs to NPTEL,
IIT Roorkee and the course instructor. The explanations, widgets, simulations and 3D models are original work.
This project is **not affiliated with or endorsed by NPTEL or IIT Roorkee**. If the rights holders
want any material removed, please open an issue and it will be removed promptly.

## Standards and references used for explanations
IEC 61851-1, SAE J1772, IEC 62196, ISO 15118, DIN 70121, GB/T 20234 and GB/T 27930, SAE J1939 (CAN identifier layout), OCPP 1.6J (Open Charge Alliance), and the Steinmetz core-loss model.
